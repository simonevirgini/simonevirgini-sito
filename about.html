Simone Virgini is a freelance illustrator who has been living between Italy and Spain since 2014. He has a Fine Arts degree from Accademia di Belle Arti di Macerata. Soon after graduation, he enrolled in the master's illustration program at Barreira Arte + Diseño in Valencia.

Today he works as an editorial illustrator for magazines, including PLANSPONSOR, PLANADVISER, Graffica, and El Pais Retina.
